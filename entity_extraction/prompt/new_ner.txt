Task: Extract clinical terms (names of lab tests, drug, AdministeredItem, outputs, procedures, microbiology findings, and physical exam findings such as pupils and temperature) from the sentence.
Remember, The clinical term must indicate that an observation or measurement was actually performed.

Extract Format:
{Extracted Entitiy: name of entity, Associated Value: value}

Extraction Rules:
1. If the target sentence contains a clinical term but no value.
Example) 
Line 11: ...vital signs: Tylenol is
Line 11: treated.

⟶ {Temperature:}

✅ 2. If the clinical term has a value
Use the value as-is, whether numeric or descriptive

If there are multiple values, extract each separately

Example 1 – Numeric:
"BP 110/70" ⟶ {BP: 110}, {BP: 70}

Example 2 – Descriptive:
"blood pressure improved" ⟶ {blood pressure: improved}
"Vancomycin was treated" ⟶ {Vancomycin: treated}

Example 3 – range:
"BP 110-120" ⟶ {BP: 110-120}

✅ 3. If the target sentence contains no clinical term and no value
Extract nothing: use {:}
Example)
Treatment for three days ⟶ {:}: There is no clinical terms (names of lab tests, drug, AdministeredItem, outputs, procedures, microbiology findings, and physical exam findings such as pupils and temperature).

Even if the context includes values, if the target sentence does not include the term, do not extract
